## Gap briefs (DRAFT pages — placeholder copy, hidden/unindexed, watermarked "DRAFT — not for publication")

Every draft follows the site pattern: problem-first opening line → concrete middle → FAQ set → closing internal link to its practice page.

**A — Road Safety Audits (08).** Buyer: government & council (defensibility), plus RSA-conditioned developers/contractors. Opening territory: when a design is challenged, "we checked it ourselves" is not a defence. Cover: audit stages (design-stage through pre-opening and existing roads); independence as the feature — the audit team must be independent of the design team, which means Sightline cannot audit its own schemes, and for this buyer that constraint is the point; site inspection; findings identified and risk-classified; recommendations; formal designer responses and close-out — the paper trail is the product. Auditor qualifications section: **leave as a marked placeholder** until accreditation is confirmed (open item D3). Do not duplicate page 07's scope; mirror its RSA-vs-site-audit FAQ in one line and link.

**B — Traffic Data Collection & Analysis (12).** Buyer: the modelling client, the council RFI, the network project needing a defensible baseline. Opening territory: a calibrated model starts with counted traffic, not assumed traffic. Cover: turning-movement counts (video), classified vehicle counts, automatic tube counts, queue observations, travel-time runs, speed data, pedestrian and cyclist counts, origin–destination/number-plate studies; how outputs feed SIDRA/Aimsun calibration and TIAs (link 09, 10, 11); deliverable formats road authorities accept; conditions that invalidate a survey (weather, school holidays, atypical events). One cross-link line each way with page 20; no shared paragraphs.

**C — Operational Traffic & Parking Management Plans (21).** Buyer: developers/architects with a condition of consent, plus the operators who inherit the document. Opening territory: the CTMP ends at completion — the consent condition doesn't. Cover (per 00-INSTRUCTIONS): internal vehicle circulation; staff, customer, resident and visitor parking; loading and delivery arrangements; waste collection; pick-up and drop-off; queue management; accessible parking; pedestrian movement; signage and line marking; responsibilities and monitoring; review and complaint procedures. Markets: car parks, schools, childcare centres, medical facilities, places of worship, shopping centres, residential developments, industrial sites. One contrast line vs page 03 (construction vs operation) plus a one-line pointer on page 03 back to this page. FAQ seeds: same as a CTMP? · when does council condition one? · who implements it once the site operates? · can it be amended later?

**D — About (28).** Carries the homepage's "prepared by engineers" claim. Structure: who Sightline is (independent practice, 2–3 plain paragraphs); the engineers (name/role/qualifications/focus — **placeholder cards only**, populated from `content/verified.ts`-style data when supplied); standards and frameworks worked to (TfNSW specifications, TCAWS, AS 1742 series, AS 2890 series, Austroads guides — factual list, no adjectives); internal checking and approval process; how an engagement runs (send the drawings → written fee proposal → delivery → issue and revision control); statistics band (verified figures only); one accurate sentence on the operating relationship if commercially relevant — never leading, per standalone positioning. No credentials, registrations, accreditations or numbers invented under any circumstances.
